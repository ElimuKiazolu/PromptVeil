Open data in the stand-in word lists

The names, places and street names PromptVeil uses for stand-ins come from
the sources below. They are selected and shortened (most frequent or most
populous first), not otherwise changed.

Faker (https://fakerjs.dev): name, company and address lists of every
locale. MIT License, see Faker-MIT.txt.

Baby names in England and Wales: from 1996, Office for National Statistics
https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/birthsdeathsandmarriages/livebirths/datasets/babynamesinenglandandwalesfrom1996
Used for: en_GB given names (girls' and boys' names by total count 1996-2025).
Retrieved: 2026-11-20.
Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0.
Licence: Open Government Licence v3.0 (https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), see OGL-3.0.txt.

https://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/products/os-open-names
Used for: en_GB localities (cities, towns, suburban areas, villages) and street names.
Retrieved: 2026-11-20.
Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2026.
